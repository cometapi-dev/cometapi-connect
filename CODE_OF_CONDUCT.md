# Code of Conduct

CometAPI Connect is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy and respect;
- focus on the work and its impact rather than people;
- welcome different backgrounds and experiences;
- accept good-faith correction; and
- protect private credentials, application profiles, and security reports.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent.

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@cometapi.com](mailto:support@cometapi.com). Security reports belong in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in a public issue.

CometAPI maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Decisions will be made in proportion to the behavior and applied consistently.
